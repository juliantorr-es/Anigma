Accessible publishing is important for ensuring that content reaches all readers, regardless of their abilities. This includes people who use screen readers, braille displays, or other assistive technologies.

THE IMPORTANCE OF STRUCTURE

Proper document structure is essential for accessibility. Headings help readers navigate through content. Lists and tables should be properly marked up.

Key Points:
- Use semantic markup
- Provide alt text for images
- Ensure proper reading order
- Test with assistive technologies
