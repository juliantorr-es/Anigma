## Summary (human-readable)

Anigma ships under the **Anigma Source-Available Non-Commercial License (Anigma-SA-NC)**. The intent is to let people read, audit, and run the software locally while reserving commercial and hosted-service rights to the author.

Layered licensing posture:
- The code in this repository is currently under Anigma-SA-NC (non-commercial).  
- Certain components may later be offered as an **open-core** package under a separate FOSS license (e.g., AGPL or similar) to support institutional adoption; that will be published separately and will not change the terms of this repository unless explicitly stated.  
- Proprietary “editions” (prebuilt binaries, integrations, advanced scouts/optimizers) remain under commercial terms.

Allowed (examples):
- Running Anigma on your personal laptop for coursework or to manage hobby projects.
- Educational use by students or instructors in a class, lab, or research setting that is non-commercial.
- Personal experimentation on your own hardware.

Not allowed without a separate commercial agreement:
- Providing Anigma as a hosted or managed service (SaaS/PaaS) to others.  
- Using Anigma in revenue-generating work, to reduce paid seats of other tools in a commercial setting, or to support paid client engagements.  
- Bundling Anigma into another product, distribution, or appliance.  
- Internal “shadow IT” deployments at companies or agencies that displace commercial tooling.

Commercial / OEM rights are reserved to the author. Separate commercial, OEM, and data-sharing agreements are available—contact the author to discuss terms.
