This Addendum (“Addendum”) supplements the applicable license or commercial agreement (“Agreement”) between Licensor and Customer regarding Customer’s use of Anigma.

## 1. Scope of Shared Artifacts
- Customer may provide, subject to this Addendum:  
  - Anonymized or pseudonymized traces and governance logs.  
  - Aggregated statistics and performance metrics.  
  - De-identified code or pattern extracts.  
  - Model deltas or distilled artifacts derived from Customer’s use.  
- **Excluded**: Personally identifiable information (PII), protected data categories, or any data prohibited by law or Customer policy without explicit written approval.

## 2. Data Protection Obligations (Licensor)
- Licensor will:  
  - Use shared artifacts solely to improve Anigma models, tooling, and services.  
  - Implement safeguards to avoid re-identification; no attempts to re-identify are permitted.  
  - Segregate artifacts as required by Customer’s data classification and the Agreement.  
  - Comply with applicable law and any DPA in force between the parties.

## 3. Rights Granted
- Customer grants Licensor a non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free right to:  
  - Use, reproduce, adapt, and create derivative works of shared artifacts to improve models and Anigma.  
  - Incorporate insights and improvements into Anigma products and services.  
- No ownership of Customer’s underlying data transfers; Licensor’s rights are limited to the artifacts provided under this Addendum.

## 4. Revocation and Survival
- Customer may revoke future sharing on written notice.  
- Revocation does not require Licensor to un-train existing models or delete already-ingested artifacts unless expressly agreed in writing.

## 5. Confidentiality
- Shared artifacts are treated as Confidential Information under the Agreement. Use is limited to the purposes stated in this Addendum.

## 6. Priority
- If there is a conflict between this Addendum and the Agreement regarding training data use, this Addendum controls for that subject matter. All other terms of the Agreement remain in effect.
