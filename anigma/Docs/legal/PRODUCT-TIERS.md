# Anigma Product Tiers (Descriptive)

[Informational only – not a contract. Pricing and exact terms are maintained outside this repo and may change. Commercial/OEM always requires a signed agreement.]

## Layered Licensing Posture
- This repository: Anigma-SA-NC (source-available, non-commercial).  
- Open core (future/parallel releases): select components may be published under a FOSS license (e.g., AGPL or similar) for institutions that need standard OSS terms.  
- Proprietary “editions”: prebuilt binaries, integrations, advanced scouts/optimizers, hosted/orchestrator bits remain commercial.

## Tier Summary

### Community Edition (Free, Non-Commercial)
- For: Personal, educational, student, disabled-individual use.  
- Allowed: Local use on personal devices for non-commercial projects and learning.  
- Disallowed: Any commercial, revenue-generating, or cost-displacing use; hosting or providing to others; internal deployments at companies/agencies.  
- Shared-learning: Optional; if enabled, subject to separate data terms.

### Individual Non-Commercial
- For: Hobbyists, power users, researchers in non-commercial contexts.  
- Allowed: Local use for non-commercial work on personal hardware.  
- Disallowed: Client work, paid services, SaaS/hosting, bundling, internal IT deployments.  
- Shared-learning: Optional; opt-in may qualify for discounts under separate data terms.

### Pro
- For: Freelancers, independent contractors, small businesses under a defined revenue/headcount threshold.  
- Allowed: Limited commercial use as specified in a Pro agreement; local-first operation.  
- Disallowed: Broad SaaS/hosting, resale, OEM bundling without upgrade.  
- Shared-learning: Optional; terms defined in Pro contract.

### Enterprise Non-Commercial
- For: Larger orgs (e.g., university departments, NGOs, public agencies) needing internal non-commercial use.  
- Allowed: Internal, non-commercial use under organizational agreements.  
- Disallowed: Revenue-generating or cost-displacing deployments, hosted services to third parties.  
- Shared-learning: Optional/negotiable; requires explicit data terms. Strict-silo is the default posture for regulated/academic environments; open-core FOSS may be used where available.

### Commercial / OEM
- For: Any bundling, SaaS/managed service, hosted offering, or commercial deployment (including internal cost-replacement at scale).  
- Allowed: As defined in a signed commercial/OEM contract.  
- Disallowed (without contract): Any of the above.  
- Shared-learning: Negotiated; may affect pricing/terms.

## Notes
- “Commercial use” includes paid client work, revenue-supporting workflows, SaaS/hosting, and internal deployments that reduce paid tooling.  
- Exact thresholds, pricing, and contract language are maintained outside this repository.  
- Data-sharing (shared-learning mode) is always optional and governed by separate agreements/annexes; strict-silo is the default for institutions unless they opt in.
