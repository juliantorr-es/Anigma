# Anigma: The Governed Accessibility Engine for Institutions

In a world increasingly reliant on digital documents, institutions like colleges, government agencies, and healthcare providers face a monumental challenge: how to make every piece of information accessible, usable, and auditable for *everyone*. Anigma rises to meet this challenge as the **Governed Accessibility Engine for Institutions**.

## The Problem: Drowning in Inaccessible Documents

Today, critical information is trapped in a firehose of PDFs, complex forms, and bureaucratic letters. This creates barriers for:

*   **Disabled Students:** Struggling to access financial aid notices, course materials, or medical reports.
*   **Clients of Agencies:** Unable to understand complex legal documents or government forms.
*   **Overwhelmed Staff:** Drowning in manual remediation tasks, trying to keep up with accessibility mandates (ADA, Section 504/508, WCAG).

Existing solutions are often piecemeal: LMS plugins for course content, static remediation vendors for PDFs, or generic cloud-based AI tools that compromise privacy and control.

## Anigma: The Solution

Anigma is an end-to-end, local-first platform designed to transform hostile documents into governed, accessible workflows.

### Our Promise:

**"Feed us your PDFs, forms, and letters; we give your disabled students and clients EPUB, braille, audio, simplified summaries, and explain-mode dashboards, all locally and auditable."**

### How Anigma Delivers:

1.  **Comprehensive Alt-Media Generation:** Automatically converts any document type into multiple accessible formats:
    *   **EPUB:** For flexible reading on e-readers and mobile devices.
    *   **Braille:** Ready for embossed output.
    *   **Audio:** High-quality text-to-speech, adaptable for different reading needs.
    *   **Simplified Summaries:** AI-generated plain-language versions of complex texts.
    *   **Explain-Mode Dashboards:** Interactive interfaces that clarify document intent and actionable steps.
2.  **Governed AI for Trust & Control:** Anigma leverages advanced, on-device Machine Learning (MLX) with robust governance. This means:
    *   **Local Processing:** All sensitive data stays within your institution's control, ensuring maximum privacy and compliance.
    *   **Auditable Workflows:** Every step of document processing and AI interaction is logged and auditable, providing compliance receipts for regulatory bodies.
    *   **Policy Enforcement:** Harmonia's Governance Model (Playbooks, Write Gate, Kill Switch) ensures AI operates within your institution's specific rules and ethical guidelines.
3.  **Institutional Ownership:** Unlike cloud-centric SaaS solutions, Anigma is designed to run on hardware you own (e.g., Anigma Node on a Mac mini). You control the data, the models, and the entire processing pipeline.

## The Anigma Difference

We don't just chase accessibility checkboxes; we empower institutions to genuinely make content usable for *all* their users, while providing the governance and auditability required in today's complex regulatory landscape. Anigma is the infrastructure that bridges the gap between inaccessible information and equitable access, turning compliance from a burden into a powerful, auditable service.
