# Market Positioning: The Anigma Niche

This document outlines the strategic position of the Anigma ecosystem relative to existing markets and technologies. It clarifies what Anigma is, but more importantly, what it is not.

The core thesis is that Anigma does not compete directly with a single category of application. Instead, it carves out a new niche by being a **local-first, MLX-backed AI engine on Apple Silicon, structured around documents, alt-media, and explain-mode, with governance baked in, and deployable as a node an institution actually owns.**

## 1. EdTech: The Institutional Engine

-   **Current Landscape:** LMS plug-ins (YuJa Panorama, Anthology Ally) that are cloud-centric, tightly coupled to the LMS, and built for course content.
-   **Anigma's Position:** Anigma sits *under* these tools as an institutional asset. LMS plugins can become clients of Anigma. While they handle course content, Anigma processes the unstructured firehose of administrative documents (financial aid letters, disability documentation, registration forms) that students, especially disabled students, drown in. We are the infra, they are the UI.

## 2. Big Tech: Apple Intelligence for Institutions

-   **Current Landscape:** Apple Intelligence offers a powerful on-device and Private Cloud Compute model for individuals. Its privacy story is strong, but it is personal, closed, and consumer-oriented.
-   **Anigma's Position:** Anigma is the institutional counterpart. It is the local, MLX-backed, open-weights-friendly stack for documents, workflows, and governance that a college, legal aid organization, or government agency can own and audit. It fills the institutional-and-governed gap that personal AI does not address.

## 3. Government Agencies: The Document Pump

-   **Current Landscape:** A "flaming trash heap" of inaccessible PDFs and websites, with a multi-decade backlog of ADA/504/508 compliance work. Current solutions are static remediation vendors who batch-fix documents.
-   **Anigma's Position:** Anigma is an in-house engine that goes beyond static remediation. It ingests arbitrary documents and produces truly *usable* outputs (EPUB, braille, audio, simplified summaries) while logging the entire process for auditors. We don't just check a box; we solve the user's problem and give the agency the receipt.

## 4. Healthcare: The Patient & Advocate Side

-   **Current Landscape:** Healthcare AI is clinician-obsessed (scribes, coding, clinical note summarization). Patient-facing tools are an afterthought.
-   **Anigma's Position:** We focus on the patient and their advocates. Anigma is a node that can live inside a clinic's network or an advocacy org's office, ingesting EOBs, denial letters, and consent forms to convert them into accessible formats and explain what they mean in plain language, all while tracking PHI access under strict policy.

## 5. Law Firms: The Client-Enablement Engine

-   **Current Landscape:** Legal AI focuses on lawyer productivity (contract review, research copilots). These are cloud-based point solutions for billable work.
-   **Anigma's Position:** Anigma is the local AI engine that sits next to the Document Management System. It ingests discovery dumps, briefs, and client documents to produce accessible formats (EPUB/audio/braille) for disabled clients and explain-mode summaries for all clients, enforcing strict governance over sensitive case data.

## 6. Non-Profits & Small Businesses: The Escape from SaaS Hell

-   **Current Landscape:** A patchwork of generic office suites, CRMs, and PDF tools, with accessibility and data governance as unaffordable afterthoughts.
-   **Anigma's Position:** Anigma offers a path to ownership. It's the "Mac mini in the closet" that turns bureaucratic garbage into usable formats and explanations, running on open-weights models, under the organization's direct control, without per-seat or per-document billing from a SaaS vendor.

## 7. Finance & Enterprise: The Unified Governance Pipeline

-   **Current Landscape:** A "zoo" of disconnected services: one vendor for PDF accessibility, another for contract AI, another for customer support bots, all glued together by PowerPoints and an internal "AI council."
-   **Anigma's Position:** Anigma is the antithesis of the zoo. It is a single, unified, governed pipeline that ingests documents and serves multiple functions (accessibility, explanation, governance, audit) for various LOB systems to plug into. It replaces SaaS duct tape with coherent infrastructure.
