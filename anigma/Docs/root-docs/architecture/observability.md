## Overview
Anigma’s observability strategy focuses on **Radical Transparency**. In a hardware-saturated environment, traditional logging is a bottleneck. We pivot to a **Saturated Spine** architecture, where evidence is generated by the hardware and reconstructed at theoretical peak speeds.

---

## 1. The Evidence Spine (Cathedral)

The **Evidence Spine** is the immutable cryptographic ledger of Anigma. Every autonomous mission contributes to the spine via **In-Kernel Heartbeats**.

### A. Heartbeat Ingestion (Eliminating the Evidence Wall)
Instead of the CPU waiting for kernels to finish and then hashing results:
1. **Generation**: The Metal/ANE DSL computes rolling BLAKE3 hashes of its execution trace.
2. **Persistence**: The GPU writes these "Hardware Heartbeats" directly to a **Saturated Logging Ring** in unified memory.
3. **Ingestion**: The `EvidenceAuthority` (Tier 2) consumes these rings in large batches, committing them to the permanent spine with zero CPU-bound hashing tax.

### B. The Spine Taxonomy
- **Mission Start**: Pre-signed mission descriptor hash.
- **Trace Heartbeat**: Rolling hash of intermediate DSL state.
- **Mission End**: Final output hash and hardware-reported duration.
- **Privacy Tombstone**: Non-identifying proof that deletion, retention expiry, legal hold, or crypto-shredding workflows ran for governed payloads.
- **Regulated Decision Record**: Hash-linked reference to assessment, human oversight, explanation, appeal, and monitoring metadata when a mission output may affect a person in a consequential context.

### C. Payload Minimization

The spine stores descriptor hashes, redacted metadata, payload references, output hashes, verifier results, and tombstone references. It must not store raw PII, PHI, secrets, full customer documents, raw sensitive prompts, or sensitive embedding vectors directly in immutable records.

## 2. Deep Trace Forensics (Observatorium)

When a security event occurs, reconstructing the "Cause-Effect Chain" across millions of entries is currently an **I/O-Bound Wall**.

### A. Saturated Forensics (The Observatorium DSL)
We implement a specialized **Forensics Megakernel** that runs on the GPU.
- **The Mission**: Scan the entire raw Binary Atlas of the Evidence Spine.
- **Parallel Search**: The GPU scans for a specific `CorrelationID` or `AnomalyHash` across gigabytes of logs in parallel.
- **Benefit**: Reconstructing a multi-agent trace moves from **Seconds (SQL)** to **Milliseconds (Saturated Scan)**.

---

## 3. Real-time Tamper Detection

Anigma maintains a "Governed Watchdog" loop in the `EvidenceAuthority`.
1. **Integrity Check**: The GPU periodically verifies the Merkle Tree of the entire spine.
2. **Gap Detection**: If a mission heartbeat is missing or the chain hash is broken, the **KillSwitch** is instantly triggered.
3. **Hardware Heartbeat Monitor**: If the GPU stops writing heartbeats for a pre-signed mission, Tier 1 assumes hardware hijacking or failure and halts the system.
