What you can do in 60 seconds.

Drop in a letter, form, or packet. Anigma groups it, labels it, pulls key dates, and shows a clean reading view. Then it generates a step-by-step guide you can follow, with every claim linked back to the source and logged in the receipt.

Drop everything if you want. Anigma digests the pile into a calm structure, shows a bird’s-eye view of the situations it recognizes, and keeps each insight tied to your original documents. It cites your files, so you never have to worry about hallucinated reality.

Why you should trust it.

Anigma never performs silent transforms or hidden network calls. Every AI action is logged with inputs, outputs, and model version. You can ask, “Show me the line it came from.”

Your processed library stays on-device, so the “making sense” layer is private by default. You control exports, sharing, and any external sync.

You can also import your own data exports from services like Facebook or Google to understand what those platforms store about you. Anigma summarizes and organizes those records locally.
